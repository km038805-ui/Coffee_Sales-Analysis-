 Coffee Sales End-to-End Data Analysis (SQL & Power BI)

 📌 Project Overview :
 his is an end-to-end data analysis project focusing on the sales performance of a coffee business across various cities in Saudi Arabia (2023-2024). The objective of this project is to uncover hidden business trends, identify top-performing products and cities, and evaluate the effectiveness of the company's discount strategy.
I utilized SQL Server for deep data exploration and answering critical business questions, followed by Power BI to build an interactive and dynamic dashboard for data storytelling.

 📌 Key Business Insights :
Through this analysis, I discovered the following actionable insights for the business

1. Top Performing Product: Colombian Coffee is the highest revenue generator, contributing 145K (22.99%) to the total revenue.
2. Most Profitable Location: The city of Hail leads the market with 77K in sales, while cities like Khobar require targeted marketing strategies to boost performance.
3. The Discount Dilemma (Crucial Insight): The data revealed a surprising trend—sales made without discounts (345K) significantly outperformed sales made with discounts (284K). This indicates strong brand loyalty and suggests that the business can safely reduce discount offers to increase overall profit margins.
